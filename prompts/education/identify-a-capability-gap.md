# Identify a capability gap

```text
I work for an education or training organisation serving fashion. Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion to identify one capability or judgement gap worth investigating with learners, employers and subject specialists.

First read methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md. Inspect available non-sensitive context about our learners, programmes, employer partners, assessment methods and existing teaching materials before asking questions.

Do not treat the survey as a curriculum specification or validation of our pedagogy, competency framework or technology platform.

Return:
1. the relevant survey finding, The Interline’s analysis and limitation;
2. the capability or judgement gap that the evidence suggests may be worth investigating;
3. what learners need to be able to do, challenge or understand — including data, workflow, trust, human review and domain expertise where relevant;
4. an exercise, assessment, employer conversation or small pilot that would test the gap rather than assume it;
5. what evidence from learners, employers and subject specialists is needed before changing a programme.

Make the proposal specific to fashion work. Do not turn it into generic AI literacy training.
```
