# Map my AI estate

```text
I work for a fashion brand or retailer. Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion to help me map the AI already in use in my organisation.

First read methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md. Inspect available non-sensitive workspace context before asking questions: project folders, policy documents, procurement records, application lists, issue trackers, meeting notes, calendars, internal instructions or connected tools. Ask only for context you cannot inspect.

Use the findings on personal tools crossing into work, fragmented AI surfaces, shallow integration, trust and mixed cost structures. Do not imply that the survey prescribes a vendor, architecture or policy.

Return:
1. the relevant survey findings and their limitations;
2. an AI-estate map with these fields: surface or tool type; user group; personal or sanctioned; data accessed; workflow and decision affected; payment model; human owner; review or escalation point; known risk;
3. the three biggest gaps in visibility, governance, data access or measurement;
4. one bounded action to take in the next month, including an owner, review point and what should be saved for reuse.

Where information is unknown, mark it unknown. Do not fill gaps with assumptions.
```

**Saved artefact:** `ai-estate-map.md` or an internal spreadsheet.
