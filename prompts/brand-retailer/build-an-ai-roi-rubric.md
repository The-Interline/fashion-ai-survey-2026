# Build an AI ROI rubric

```text
I work for a fashion brand or retailer. Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion to build an ROI rubric for one live AI workflow.

First read methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md. Inspect available non-sensitive workspace context first. Ask only for the workflow, owner or data you cannot determine.

Use the finding that AI costs are fragmented across licences, tokens or usage charges, hybrid pricing, integration work and human review. Do not imply that the survey gives us a universal ROI threshold.

Return:
1. the relevant survey findings and limitations;
2. a one-page workflow definition: user, job to be done, decision affected, data used, human owner and review point;
3. the full cost base: licence, token or usage charges, integration, implementation, training, human review and failure handling;
4. baseline and target measures: time, quality, error, cost, adoption, risk and user trust;
5. stop, continue and revise criteria;
6. a review cadence and the evidence that should be saved after each cycle.

Do not count a workflow as successful merely because usage rises or output arrives faster.
```

**Saved artefact:** `ai-roi-rubric.md` or a controlled internal spreadsheet.
