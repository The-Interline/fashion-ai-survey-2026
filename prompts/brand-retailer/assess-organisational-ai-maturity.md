# Assess organisational AI maturity

```text
I work for a fashion brand or retailer and want to know whether we are ahead, behind or on par with the industry on AI maturity.

Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion. First read methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md. Inspect available non-sensitive workspace context before asking questions.

Do not give an industry-ranking verdict. This companion is a directional, self-selecting survey, not a representative maturity index, and you cannot assess our organisation without seeing our workflows.

Instead:
1. explain what the survey can and cannot establish about maturity;
2. assess available evidence against eight internal dimensions: AI-estate visibility, data integration, decision trust, workflow adoption, cost structure, ROI discipline, human review and workforce capability;
3. label the result an internal diagnostic, not an external benchmark;
4. for each dimension, identify the evidence seen, the evidence missing, the current risk and one question for the owner;
5. recommend one bounded diagnostic action;
6. state what would be needed for a genuine external benchmark: comparable peers, consistent definitions, workflow-level evidence and independent validation.

Do not name vendors, recommend a technology stack or imply that the survey ranks us against the market.
```
