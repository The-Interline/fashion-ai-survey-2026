# Test the non-virtuous loop

```text
I work for a fashion brand or retailer. Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion to test whether we have the non-virtuous loop described in *Arm’s Length AI*.

The hypothesis is: AI is kept outside live systems; missing context creates weak or ungrounded output; people do not trust that output for consequential work; the lack of trust then keeps AI at arm’s length from the systems and data that could make it more useful.

First read methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md. Inspect available non-sensitive workspace context before asking questions.

Treat this as a hypothesis, not a causal fact proven by the survey.

Return:
1. the relevant survey findings, The Interline’s analysis and their limitations;
2. evidence for and against each stage of the loop in our organisation;
3. one workflow where a bounded, reversible integration test could be run safely;
4. the minimum data needed, the data that must remain excluded, the human owner and the review or escalation point;
5. success and failure criteria, including accuracy, usefulness, time saved, cost and trust;
6. what we should save as a reusable operating rule if the test works.

Do not recommend connecting AI to mission-critical systems without a bounded test, access controls and explicit human accountability.
```
