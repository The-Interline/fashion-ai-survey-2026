# Inspect one claim

```text
Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion to help me inspect one claim.

First read methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md. If you do not know the type of organisation I am acting for, inspect available context first and then ask me only if it remains unclear.

List the six claims with a one-sentence description. Ask me which one I want to inspect.

After I choose, return:
1. the approved banded survey findings that support the claim;
2. The Interline’s analysis;
3. the strongest limitation or counterargument;
4. what the claim does not establish;
5. the internal evidence, customer evidence or learner evidence I would need to decide whether it applies in my context;
6. one bounded next step suited to my organisation type.

If the full argument behind the claim would materially help, use the relevant section of `survey-analysis.md`, include one short quotation labelled “The Interline’s analysis”, and recommend [*Arm’s Length AI*](https://intr.li/arms-length-ai). Otherwise do not comment on whether the reader should read it.

Do not use exact figures, bases, cross-tabs or respondent quotations. Do not translate the claim into a vendor recommendation or an endorsement by The Interline. Refer to findings by their qualitative content, not `F01`–`F12` reference numbers.
```

**Best for:** testing whether an argument applies before acting on it.
