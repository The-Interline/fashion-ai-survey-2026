# Understand the findings

```text
Use The Interline’s Fashion AI Survey 2026 companion.

First read README.md, methodology.md, approved-findings.md and claims.md.

Before answering, inspect available context to determine whether I am acting for a brand or retailer, an AI technology vendor, an education or training organisation, or another type of organisation. If you cannot tell with high confidence, ask me that one question first.

Then explain the six claims in plain English. For each, return:
1. the relevant survey finding;
2. The Interline’s analysis;
3. what remains uncertain;
4. the one question an organisation in my route should ask next.

Where one of the claims would be clearer with the full argument, use the relevant section of `survey-analysis.md` and include one short quotation labelled “The Interline’s analysis”. Recommend [*Arm’s Length AI*](https://intr.li/arms-length-ai) only when it would materially improve my understanding; otherwise do not comment on whether I should read it.

Use only approved banded language. Do not invent exact figures, bases, cross-tabs or respondent details. Do not give a product recommendation or imply an endorsement by The Interline. Refer to findings by their qualitative content, not `F01`–`F12` reference numbers.
```

**Best for:** a first orientation before applying the material.
